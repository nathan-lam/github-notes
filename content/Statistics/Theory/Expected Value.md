

# Definition
A weighted sum of each possible outcome weighted by the probability

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{For Discrete Random Variables: } E(X) &= \sum xP(X=x)\\
\text{For Continuous Random Variables: } E(X) &= \int^\infty_{-\infty} x P(X=x) dx\\
\end{aligned}
$$
Here $x$ is a possible event for $X$ and $P(X=x)$ is the probability and the weight of that event


# Example
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Let }X &\sim  Bernoulli(0.7)\\
E(X) &= 1 \cdot P(\text{Prob of being 1}) + 0 \cdot P(\text{Prob of being 0})\\
&= 1 \cdot 0.7 + 0 \cdot (1-0.7)\\
&= 0.7
\end{aligned}
$$
