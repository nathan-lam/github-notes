
The sample average converges to the true average assuming [[Independent and Identically Distributed]]

$$
\lim_{n \rightarrow\infty} P (|\hat{p}-p| < \epsilon) = 1
$$
$\hat{p}$ depends on $n$, which converges to $p$ with more samples.
