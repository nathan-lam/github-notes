
# Statement

The ordinary least squares estimator will have the property of having the lowest variance among other linear [[Unbiased|unbiased]] estimators assuming errors are uncorrelated with mean 0 and equal variances



Errors do not need to me [[Normal]] or [[Independent and Identically Distributed|iid]], just uncorrelated with mean zero and [[homoscedastic]] finite variance.

This is not a statement of the absolute lowest variance as biased estimators could achieve lower variance.
