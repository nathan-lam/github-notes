# Definition
A metric that estimates the [[Effect Size]] of a difference in two groups when performing a [[Hypothesis Tests|hypothesis test]]

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cohen's d} &= \frac{\bar{x}_1-\bar{x}_2}{\sqrt{\frac{s^2_1+s^2_2}{2}}}\\
\bar{x}_i &= \text{ith sample mean}\\
s^2_i &= \text{ith sample variance}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
