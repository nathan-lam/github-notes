Fitting a polynomial to data

# Definition
Suppose we fit a degree 3 polynomial, we assume a model of the form
$$
y = \beta_{0}+ \beta_{1} x+\beta_{2}x^{2}+ \beta_{3}x^{3}+\epsilon
$$


# Assumptions
- There is a kth degree polynomial relationship
- Extrapolation is not being conducted
	- Polynomials explode outside their sample space


# Related
This is an example of [[Generalized Additive Models]] where a single polynomial is used.
