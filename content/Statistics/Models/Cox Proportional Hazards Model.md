# Definition
A [[Survival Analysis]] model to determine how factors contribute to an event happening.

$$
\begin{aligned}
h(t|X) = h_0(t) \cdot e^{\beta_1X_1 + \beta_2X_2+\dots+\beta_pX_p}\\
X_i = \text{variables}\\
\beta_i = \text{Regression coef}\\
h(t|X)=\text{Hazard function at time t given X}\\
h_0(t) = \text{Baseline hazard function}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
