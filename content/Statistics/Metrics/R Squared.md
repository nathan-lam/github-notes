# Definition
A criterion used to evaluate if a model explains the data

$$
\begin{aligned}
R^{2}&= 1 - \frac{\text{Error sum of squares}}{\text{Total sum of Squares}}\\
&= 1-\frac{\sum(y-\hat{y})^2}{\sum\limits(\bar{y}-\hat{y})^2}\\
&= 1 - \frac{var(errors)}{var(y)}\\
\end{aligned}
$$

Error sum of squares quantifies how much the model does NOT capture




# Variations
R squared is prone to inflating number of variables and sample size
