A distance measure between probability distributions

# Definition

$$
D_{KL}(P|Q) = \sum_{x}P(x) log(\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)})
$$
x is the sample space 
The difference is measured through the ratio of the true distribution, $P(x)$, and similar distribution, $Q(x)$
