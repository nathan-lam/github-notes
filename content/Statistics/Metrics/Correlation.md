An association between numbers that change together


# Pearson 
The linear relationship between 2 numerical variables

$$
\rho_{X,Y} = \frac{cov(X,Y)}{\sigma_{x}\sigma_{y}}
$$
Treating $X,Y$ as vectors can be calculated from the dot product
$$
cos \theta = \rho_{X,Y}
$$


# Speareman 
The increasing relationship between variables
- Utilizing ranks to more broadly describe increasing/decreasing together
- Does not assume a linear relationship

It is defined as the Pearson correlation of the ranks
