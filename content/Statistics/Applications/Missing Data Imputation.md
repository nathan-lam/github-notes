
Data can be missing from a variety of sources and it is common that we have incomplete data.


Any form of imputation must be documented for transparency as these filled in data may badly influence the results

# Methods of Imputation
- Impute with an average
- Smoothing (for time series)
	- Rolling average
	- Exponential smoothing
- Bootstrapping (simulate the data distribution)
	- Resample bootstrapping
	- [[EM Algorithm]]
