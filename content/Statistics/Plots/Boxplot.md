A visualization of how spread the data is

# Definition
Plots the 4 Quartiles of a dataset

IQR = Q3-Q1
# Usage
One test for outliers is to remove points outside \[Q1-1.5 IQR, Q3 + 1.5 IQR\]

It's a way saying to remove points past 2.5 std out
