# Definition
A property that indicates the probability is the same across time. This implies the property of no trend/seasonality and [[Homoscedasticity|homoscadastic]]



# Types

## Weakly Stationary
Constant Mean
Finite variance in time
Autocorrelation depends only on h (independent in time)

## White Noise
Mean = 0
finite variance in time
uncorrelated

## IID Noise (Strongly Stationary)
White noise + IID samples

## Gaussian Noise
IID Noise + Normally distributed
