Data points that don't follow the general trend

# Definition
Data points that are wildly different from the data. These are points of pain for modeling as they can throw off the predictions



Should outliers be removed?
- Remove if the process that made the outlier is not normal
- Remove if the outliers distracts the model
Essentially, keep outliers if they are legitimate and still be considered

# Variations

## High Leverage
Points with extreme input values
