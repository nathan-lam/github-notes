# Definition
A stochastic process of a taking random steps in space.

The outcome of a step is determined by a chosen probability distribution.

The sequence is dependent on the previous value, but the step size is sampled from a distribution.

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_n = S_{n-1} + X_n\\
X_i \sim D
\end{aligned}
$$
