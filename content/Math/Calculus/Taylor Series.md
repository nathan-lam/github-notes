# Definition
A representation of any function as an infinite polynomial sum

The taylor series centered around $a$ is:
$$
f(x)=\sum^\infty_{n=0} \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n
$$

if a = 0, then it is called a [[Maclaurin Series]]

## Approximations
A truncated taylor series can be used to approximate a functioon as along as the higher order terms are negligible.

Examples
- $sin x \approx x, \text{for small }x$
