# Definition

A condition where $\sum^\infty_{n=0} |f_n(x)|$ sums to a finite value.


If a sum absolutely converges, then the sum converges
- $\sum^\infty_{n=0} |f_n(x)| \text{ converges} \implies \sum^\infty_{n=0} f_n(x) \text{ converges}$
