# Definition
If a limit is of an [[Indeterminate Form]], then representing the limit as a ratios of functions, the limit is the same as the derivitives of the numerator and denominator.
$$\lim_{x\to c} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim_{x\to c}\frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}$$
