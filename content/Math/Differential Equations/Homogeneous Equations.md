# Definition
[[Differential Equations]] that have a constant part equal to 0


$$
\begin{aligned}
y'' + y'+y&=0 ,\text{ is homogeneous}\\
y'' + y'+y-3 &= 0, \text{ is nonhomogeneous}
\end{aligned}
$$


Every nonhomogenous differential equation still also have a homogeneous solution.
