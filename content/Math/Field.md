# Definition
A set F with two operations, $(\times, +)$ that satisfy the axioms
- F is closed under $\times$ and $+$
- $\times$ and $+$ are [[commutative]] respectively
- $\times$ and $+$ are [[associative]] respectively
- $\times$ and $+$ each have an identity and a corresponding inverse element
	- additive identity is 0
	- multiplicative identity is 1
	- additive inverse of $a$ is $-a$
	- multiplicative inverse of $a$ is $a^{-1}$ except 0
- $\times$ distributes over $+$
	- [[Distributive]] property, an interaction between the operations


# Examples
- Vector Fields
- Temperature
- [[Rational Numbers]]
- [[Real Numbers]]

Not a field
- Integer Numbers
