
# Definition
An infinite sum of terms with a common factor

$$
\begin{aligned}
S= \sum^\infty_{n=0} r^n\\
rS = \sum^\infty_{n=0} r^{n+1}\\
S-rS = 1\\
S = \frac{1}{1-r}\\
\therefore r\in (-1,1) \implies \sum^\infty_{n=0} r^n = \frac{1}{1-r}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
