Operating on vector functions, not derivatives as a linear operator


Look at the ith element is see how the derivative interacts
$$
\begin{align}
f(\vec{\theta}) &= \vec{c}^{T}\vec{\theta}= \sum^n_{i=1}c_i\theta_{i}\\
\frac{\partial f(\vec{\theta})}{\partial \vec{\theta}} &= \vec{c} 
\end{align}
$$

$$
\begin{align}
f(\vec{\theta}) &= \vec{\theta}^TA\vec{\theta}= \sum^n_{i=1}a_i\theta_{i}^2\\
\frac{\partial f(\vec{\theta})}{\partial \vec{\theta}} &= 2A\vec{\theta} =  \sum^n_{i=1}2a_i\theta_{i}\\
\end{align}
$$
