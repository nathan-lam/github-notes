# Definition
A property between two sets that do not share any common elements.
$$S_1 \cap S_2 = \emptyset \implies S_1 \text{ and } S_2 \text{ are disjoint}$$

## n-wise Disjoint
A collection of sets that does not share any $n$ elements

Example:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\{1,2,3\} \text{ is pairwise disjoint to } \{3,4,5\}\\
&\{1,2,3\} \text{ is not pairwise disjoint to } \{2,3,4\} \text{because they share }\{2,3\}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
