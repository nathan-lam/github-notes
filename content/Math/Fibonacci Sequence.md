# Definition
A [[Recurrence Relations|recursive relation]] where $F_n = F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$


This can be used to model grow in a population


# Notes

## Closed formed Formula
This sequence uses the golden ratio as any Fibonacci number can be express as a base of the [[Golden Ratio]]
This becomes the Binet Formula
$$F_n = \frac{\phi^n-(-\phi)^{-n}}{\sqrt{5}}$$

## Basis
Like how numbers are typically written as powers of 10, the fibonacci numbers can represent any number, though not uniquely.

# Related
- The [[Generating Functions|generating function]] that creates the fibonacci sequence is $$\frac{x}{1-x-x^2}$$
