

# Definition
An operator that maps two vectors to a scalar quantity

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1\\2\\3
\end{bmatrix}
\cdot
\begin{bmatrix}
4\\5\\6
\end{bmatrix}
= 1\cdot4 + 2\cdot5+3\cdot6
$$
