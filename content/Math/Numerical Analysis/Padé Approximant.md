

# Definition
A function approximation using a ratio of polynomials.

A function $f(x)$ is approximated as a ratio of polynomials, $\frac{P_n(x)}{Q_m(x)}$ , where (n,m) represent the degree of the polynomial


Because this is a ratio, $\lim_{x\rightarrow\infty} \frac{P_n(x)}{Q_m(x)} = 0$
- this differs from [[Taylor Series]] approximation where the absolute limit becomes $\infty$
