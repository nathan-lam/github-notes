# Definition

$$
\begin{aligned}
e = \lim_{n\rightarrow\infty} (1+\frac{1}{n})^n\\
e = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!}
\end{aligned}
$$

$e$ is the natural number to describe exponential growth as it always grows at the same rate.
- $\frac{d}{dx} e^x = e^x$
