# Definition

A relationship between the ratio and angles of a triangle.


Given a diagram:

![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a5/Law_of_sines_%28simple%29.svg/250px-Law_of_sines_%28simple%29.svg.png)

$$
\frac{a}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{b}{\sin\beta} = \frac{c}{\sin\gamma} = 2R
$$
