# Definition


$$
\begin{aligned}
f(\alpha x) &= \alpha f(x) \forall\alpha\\
f(x+y) &= f(x) + f(y)\\
f(\alpha x+\beta y) &= \alpha f(x) + \beta f(y)
\end{aligned}
$$
