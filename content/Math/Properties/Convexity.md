# Definition
A property of a curve in relation to a straight line.

A function is considered convex if
$$
f(tx_1+(1-t)x_2) \leq t f(x_1) + (1-t)f(x_2)\forall t\in[0,1], \forall x
$$
In english, for any straight line, connecting points on a curve, the straight line is always greater or equal to the curve.
