# Definition
For any prime number $p$ and any integer $a$ , then $a^p-a$ is a multiple of $p$

$$a^p \equiv a (\text{mod }p)$$
or
$$a^{p-1} \equiv 1 (\text{mod }p)$$

All primes will satisfy this statement, but not exclusively primes.
- Numbers that are not prime but satisfy this relation are [[pseudo primes]]
