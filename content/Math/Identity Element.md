# Definition
An element that does that does nothing under an operation.

$5\cdot 1 = 5$
- 1 does not change 5 under multiplication

# Examples
- 1 is a multiplicative identity
- 0 is an additive identity
- $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$ is the matrix multiplication identity
