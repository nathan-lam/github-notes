# Proof by Contradiction
Given a statement $P$
Assume the opposite, $\neg P$, is true
Proceed by making a trail of statements until a contradition is made.
Because the crux relies on $\neg P$ and $\neg P$ gives a false statement, that implies the opposite is true or $P$ is demonstrated to be true
