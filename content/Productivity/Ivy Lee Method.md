# Definition
A methodology of keeping goals in mind.

It is broken down as the following
1. At the end of the day, write the 6 most important tasks to get done.
2. Rank each tasks by importance
3. Address the tasks by importance the next day
4. Carry over unfinished tasks over to the next draft of 6 most important tasks

A list of tasks can be greater than 6, but the goal is to know exactly what is the most important thing to be working on.
