# Definition
An equation that relates conditions under a hypothetical ideal gas.

$$
\begin{aligned}
pV &= nRT\\
p &= \text{Absolute pressure of the gas}\\
V &= \text{Volume of the gas}\\
n &= \text{Amount of gas (based on number of moles)}\\
R &= \text{Universal gas constant}\\
T &= \text{Absolute temperature of the gas}\\
\end{aligned}
$$
