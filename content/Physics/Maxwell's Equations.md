## [[Gauss's Law]]
$$\nabla\cdot E = \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0} $$

## [[Gauss's Law for magnetism]]
$$\nabla\cdot B = 0$$


## [[Faraday's Law]]
$$\nabla\times E = -\frac{\partial B}{\partial t} $$

## [[Ampère-Maxwell Law]]
$$ \nabla\times B = \mu_0(J+\epsilon_0\frac{\partial E}{\partial t}) $$
