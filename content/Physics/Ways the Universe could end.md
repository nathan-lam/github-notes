# Scenarios

## [[Heat Death]]
The universe will expand until everything is so spread out that energy everywhere is at an equilibrium.


## Big Crunch
The universe will come to a point in time where [[Cosmic Inflation]] can no longer overcome gravity, resulting in everything snapping back into a singularity.

This supports the idea of a [[Cyclical Universe]]
