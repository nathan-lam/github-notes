# Definition
A physical law describing temperature and entropy.


## Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics
```
If two systems are both in thermal equilibrium with a third system, then they are in thermal equilibrium with each other.
```

A statement relating 2 systems can share the same temperature with a common object.

## First Law of Thermodynamics
```
Energy stays constant in a closed system.
```

This is a statement regarding the [[Law of Conservation of Energy]] as if energy cannot be created or destroyed, it must change in nature.
## Second Law of Thermodynamics
```
Natural processes trend towards homogeneity.
```

This is a statement about [[Entropy]] as energy trends towards equilibrium.

## Third Law of Thermodynamics
```
A system's energy approaches a constant value when approaching absolute zero.
```

This is a statement about the existence of the coldest temperature, [[Absolute Zero]].
