# Definition
Forces that the universe will always exert onto objects.


Forces tend to have a force carrying particle to exert that force.


# Types


## Gravity
An illusion of a force that attracts mass together and is a result of the curvature of space instead of a particle.

## [[Electromagnetism]]
A force that attracts charges away or together.

The force carrying particle is the photon.


## [[Strong Force]]
A force that attract protons together into a nucleus of an atom.
