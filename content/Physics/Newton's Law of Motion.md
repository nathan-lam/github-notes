# Definition
Three natural laws that explain the relationship between motion and forces.

These were laws first described by Isaac Newton, but are later known as good approximations of quantum mechanics and relativity.

## First Law of Motion
```
A body remains at rest, or in motion at a constant speed in a straight line, unless it is acted upon by a force.
```

Speed does not change unless there is something to change its speed.

This describes the concept of [[Inertia]]

## Second Law of Motion
```
At any instant of time, the net force on a body is equal to the body's acceleration multiplied by its mass or, equivalently, the rate at which the body's momentum is changing with time.
```
$$Force = Mass \times Acceleration$$
Note that the time derivative of momentum = acceleration

## Third Law of Motion
```
If two bodies exert forces on each other, these forces have the same magnitude but opposite directions
```

Forces have equal and opposite reactions
