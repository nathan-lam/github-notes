# Definition
E-commerce platforms have a certain type of demographic that a seller interacts with. 


There are different types of relationships.
- Whales
	- The business relies on a few big spenders
	- This is common for niche markets
	- Learning how to satisfy big spenders becomes a priority
	- Can be risky if big spenders move else where
- Diversifying
	- Introducing to more people
	- This is common to sell to broad audiences.
	- Becomes a numbers game, more impressions the better
- Seasonality
	- Demand for products come in waves.
		- Typically from holidays or special events.
	- 

# Questions to ask

It is useful to know the type of demographic as appealing to them makes them more likely to be returning customers.
