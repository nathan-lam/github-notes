# Definition
A debate whether mathematics is created or discovered.


This is a semantic debate to ask if mathematics already existed before their descriptions.

# Arguments For "Created"
- Math is a tool and we shape it to fit the problem we solve
	- like fitting a linear model to describe a ball being thrown


# Arguments For "Discovered"
The unreasonable effectiveness of math
- Math is so good at describing reality that the math can lead to new discoveries of nature.
	- Neptune was discovered by investigating weird orbit patterns
	- Relativity predicted black holes.


# My opinion
The tools of math, like calculus and linear algebra were created. But the relationships of nature are discovered, we just mold our tools to describe that relationship.
