# Definition
A pragmatic argument in favor to believe in God.

|                     | Belief in God   | No Belief in God |
| ------------------- | --------------- | ---------------- |
| God Exists          | Infinite reward | Nothing          |
| God does not Exists | Nothing         | Infinite torture |

The argument stems from the fact that at worst, belief in God will not be punishing.


# Issues

## False Dichotomy
This argument fails to consider other views as this is an all or nothing perspective.

A more fair comparison would be to include all other religions.


## Faith
Belief in God, especially in Christianity, is qualified by their follower's devotion in blind faith.

This argument comes from pragmatism, not faith, and may still count as a sin.
