# Definition
A flavor of [[Intelligent Design]] applied to the laws of physics.
```
The fundamental constants of the universe are so specific that if it was slightly modified, then life could never have existed.
```

This ignores that there could be other different but valid configurations, life a local minima among any other local minima.

This falls under the anthropic bias as the universe(s) that can exist are the only ones that can have observers. 
- A universe that cannot sustain life cannot be observed by the inhabitants.
